About Chinese Home Kitchen

Chinese Home Kitchen is a small collection of recipes that feel like home.

We focus on dishes that are simple to cook, warm to share, and full of story. Every recipe comes with a short story, clear ingredients, and step-by-step instructions — so you can cook with confidence, even on a busy weeknight.

Whether you grew up eating these dishes or are trying them for the first time, we hope this site feels like sitting at a family table.
