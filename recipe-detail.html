Sichuan Classic

Mapo Tofu

Medium • 30 min • Serves 4

Soft tofu simmered in a spicy, numbing sauce with ground pork and Sichuan peppercorns.

01

The Story

Mapo tofu comes from Chengdu, Sichuan. Its name means "pockmarked grandmother's tofu," named after the woman who first made it. The dish is famous for its balance of spicy, numbing, and savory flavors.

02

Ingredients

  • 1 block soft tofu
  • 100g ground pork
  • 2 tbsp doubanjiang
  • 1 tsp Sichuan peppercorns
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp ginger, minced
  • 2 green onions, chopped
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch slurry
03

Preparation

  1. Drain the tofu and cut into 1-inch cubes.
  2. Mince the garlic and ginger.
  3. Mix cornstarch with water for the slurry.
  4. Toast and grind the Sichuan peppercorns.
04

Cooking

  1. Heat oil, add garlic and ginger, stir until fragrant.
  2. Add ground pork, cook until browned.
  3. Add doubanjiang, stir for 1 minute.
  4. Add water and soy sauce, bring to a simmer.
  5. Gently slide in tofu cubes, simmer 5 minutes.
  6. Thicken with cornstarch slurry.
  7. Finish with Sichuan pepper and green onions.
05

Ready to Serve

Serve over steamed white rice. Pairs well with a light cucumber salad or stir-fried bok choy.

Need some help?

Tap a question to see a friendly cooking tip.

What can I substitute for tofu?

You can use silken tofu, soft egg tofu, or even scrambled eggs for a different texture.

How can I make this less spicy?

Reduce the doubanjiang to 1 tsp and skip the Sichuan peppercorns.

What can I serve with this dish?

Steamed jasmine rice, a light cucumber salad, or stir-fried bok choy.
